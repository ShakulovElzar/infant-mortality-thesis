# University of Messina L-31 fit (checked 2026-11-09)

The student's official degree is **Informatica L-31, curriculum Data Analysis**, rather than a separate L-31 degree named Data Analysis. The [2025 course regulation](https://www.unime.it/sites/default/files/2025-08/Regolamento%20didattico%20informatica%20L-31%20-%20Versione%20dell%2701%20Agosto%202025.pdf), arts. 2 and 5 and annex 2, describes machine learning, statistics, visualization, programming, databases, data mining, and AI. The annex is explicitly for the **2025–26 entry cohort**; the student's enrollment year must be checked before using its detailed study plan. The [current course page](https://www.unime.it/didattica/corsi-di-laurea-triennali-e-ciclo-unico/informatica) confirms the two curricula and their aims.

Article 15 of the 2025 regulation permits a written final project presented and discussed publicly. It requires a signed topic assignment **at least 90 days before the graduation session**, and upload of the approved work **at least seven working days before the exam**. The workload is tied to the credits allocated to the final exam (the 2025–26 annex assigns 4 CFU preparation plus 2 CFU exam). The commission can award up to seven evaluation points for quality, mastery, connection to course topics, coherent conclusions, and presentation. The older [2021 SUA](https://mift.unime.it/sites/dip06/files/2024-01/SUA_L31_Informatica_2021.pdf) explicitly describes an applied/experimental option; this is corroborating history, not a substitute for the student's applicable regulation. The [course graduation page](https://informatica.cdl.unime.it/it/laurearsi) points to a department-level exam regulation valid across cohorts; obtain that document and confirm with the supervisor before fixing administrative dates.

The [2023 Informatica regulation, Article 13(3–4)](https://informatica.cdl.unime.it/sites/cdl36/files/2023-09/L-31_INFORMATICA_Regolamento_Didattico_REV%5B2023%5D.pdf) likewise requires the thesis-assignment application countersigned by the supervisor at least 90 days before the beginning of the first useful graduation session; the **incoming protocol date** controls. The student names the supervisor and topic in the application. Thus an exploratory email to a professor does not satisfy the 90-day rule. The text does not say the administration must finish a later approval within 90 days, but it does require the countersigned application to be formally received/protocolled by then. The [Informatica student-procedures page](https://informatica.cdl.unime.it/it/studiare/pratiche-studenti) links the online 'Richiesta elaborato finale' form; its exact routing, signature and protocol mechanics were not verified. Ask the course office for the protocol number/date after submission.

For a planned **March 2027** graduation, the [Informatica session page](https://informatica.cdl.unime.it/it/laurearsi/appelli-di-laurea) currently lists 2026 dates only, so the exact 2027 date is unconfirmed. A session on 9 March would put the 90-day topic-assignment deadline on **9 December 2026**; an earlier session would move it earlier. Confirm the applicable cohort rule and exact session with the course office now. Do not wait for a research-dataset access decision before filing an approved topic.

An applied thesis with a modest original experiment, reproducible code, and a short interpretive report fits the curriculum. A 12-week research plan is a **planning estimate**, not a university workload rule. The methodological aim should be stronger than the software demo: label provenance, spatial or temporal holdout, baseline comparison, and uncertainty.

Potential supervisor routes are the [MIFT department](https://mift.unime.it/it) and its Informatica/Data Analysis teaching staff, plus the [Future Computing Research Laboratory](https://rc.fcrlab.unime.it/) for machine learning. The public sources inspected did not establish a named supervisor specifically offering satellite agriculture theses. The appropriate supervisor should be selected after checking current teaching and research profiles and discussing data access.
